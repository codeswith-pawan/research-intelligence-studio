            RESEARCH INTELLIGENCE STUDIO

            Academic Review & Evidence Grounding Guidelines

            8-Dimension Evaluation Framework

            Research manuscripts are evaluated across 8 academic criteria using a calibrated heuristic scoring engine:

            1. Originality & Novelty: Uniqueness of hypotheses, conceptual framing, and contributions.
            2. Methodological Rigor: Soundness of mathematical formulation, validation, and baselines.
            3. Empirical Evidence: Verifiability of quantitative claims and benchmark results.
            4. Related Work: Comprehensive scholarly positioning and citation attribution.
            5. Clarity & Structure: Scholarly exposition, flow, readability, and reproducibility.
            6. Statistical Validity: Proper reporting of variance, confidence, and sample size.
            7. Ethical Considerations: Data privacy, compute reporting, and research safety.
            8. Limitations & Future Work: Candid disclosure of boundary constraints.

            Strict Quotation Grounding Safety Gate

            Every extracted claim must be verbatim-grounded in the original source manuscript PDF/DOCX. Unverified quotes trigger a Needs Manual Review safety gate to prevent LLM hallucinations from influencing the academic review.
